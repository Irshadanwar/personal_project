About Me

Hello! I'm Zainab Anwar, an 8th-grade student with a passion for sports, writing, and art. Whether it’s playing basketball, painting, or writing heartfelt stories, I put my whole heart into it. Inspired by the charm and elegance of K-Dramas, I aim to live with passion, grace, and creativity.

Basketball

Basketball is my escape, my power zone. I love how it teaches me strength, strategy, and teamwork. One day, I dream of playing on the national level.

🏀 Practicing daily helps me stay focused and disciplined.

🌟 I admire athletes who are graceful and fierce at the same time.

Writing

I love writing poems and stories about friendship, growth, and dreams. It’s how I share my heart with the world.

📖 Journaling gives me clarity and comfort.

📝 “Dreams are made of stories we dare to write.” — Me 💕

K-Drama

I’m a huge fan of K-Dramas! They inspire me with their aesthetics, values, and emotions. I often draw or write inspired by shows like "Crash Landing on You" and "Goblin."

Painting

Painting brings me peace and freedom. I love mixing colors and painting whatever I feel—no rules, just vibes 💗

🎨 Soft pastels and florals are my favorite themes.

🖌️ Each painting tells a piece of my story.
